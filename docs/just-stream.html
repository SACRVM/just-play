The broadcaster · how it works

JUST STREAM

How JUST STREAM works — capturing your sound, cleaning it up and putting it on air — in plain language.

What it does

JUST STREAM takes a live sound source, runs it through the same tone tools as the player, and broadcasts it to an Icecast server. The point: get your set on air without a tangle of virtual cables.

Where the sound comes from — App or Device

App (the headline trick)

Instead of grabbing a whole sound card, JUST STREAM can grab the audio of ONE application — your DJ software, a browser tab, anything. Only that app goes on air; nothing else on your PC leaks in.

Device

Or pick any sound card / audio device as the source, straight from a dropdown.

ASIO note: If your DJ software sends its sound out over ASIO or "WASAPI exclusive", Windows hides it from other apps — so app-capture can't hear it (you'd get silence). Fix: set that app's output to a normal (shared) Windows device, or use a Device source instead. JUST STREAM shows a note when this might be the case.

Keeping your cue off the air — Broadcast Channels

DJ gear usually sends the Master out on one pair of channels and your private headphone cue on another. JUST STREAM defaults to broadcasting Channels 1-2 — on that gear, the Master — so your cue never goes out by accident. If your setup routes the Master elsewhere, you can pick Channels 3-4 or the full mix.

The broadcast DSP

The same rack as the player — EQ, TILT, PUNCH and the Limiter — with the same one-click genre presets (Electronic, Hard, Rock, Neutral). The one difference: JUST STREAM leans the limiter a notch louder, because a live stream has to stay competitively loud and you can't ride the gain mid-mix. For what each control actually does, see the JUST PLAY page. As in the player, right-click a preset to replace it with your current settings, rename, or delete it.

MP3 or Opus

Stream in MP3 (works everywhere) or Opus (smaller for the same quality). Pick the bitrate: 128, 192, 256 or 320 kbps. One catch: live "now playing" song titles only ride on MP3 — Opus can't carry them.

Server profiles

Save as many Icecast servers as you like — host, mount, login, MP3 or Opus, optional TLS. Switch between them from the top.

Watch your sound — meters & spectrum

A true-peak level meter with limiter lights shows how hard you're pushing, and the spectrum view shows your sound before vs after the DSP (DRY vs WET) against the reference curve — so you can see what you're putting out, not just hear it.

Mini mode

Shrink JUST STREAM to a compact on-air strip that stays out of the way while you mix.
